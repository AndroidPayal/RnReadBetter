# ReadBetter by The ReadBetter Co.

This project implements a scientific and structured reading method that gives readers the freedom to choose books they enjoy, helps them discover new books suitable to their reading level, and encourages regular reading habits.

The system focuses on improving reader engagement through personalization and progressive reading.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Allow readers to freely choose books based on their interests
- Recommend new books matching the reader’s reading level
- Encourage consistent and regular reading habits
- Support reading development through a structured method

---

## 📚 Key Features
- Reader-driven book selection
- Reading level assessment and categorization
- Personalized book recommendations
- Encouragement of daily and regular reading
- Structured and scientific reading approach

---

## 🛠️ Methodology
The project follows a scientific reading approach based on:
- Reader interest analysis
- Reading level identification
- Progressive content recommendation
- Habit-building through regular engagement

This method ensures that readers remain motivated while continuously improving their reading skills.

---

## 🧠 Project Purpose
This project was designed to:
- Promote reading habits among learners
- Support personalized learning experiences
- Apply analytical thinking to educational challenges
- Bridge technology and education through structured systems

- Educators and teachers
- Reading programs and educational institutions
